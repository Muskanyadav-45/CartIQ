# 🛒 CartIQ – Intelligent Customer Segmentation & Shopping Analytics System

CartIQ is an intelligent customer segmentation and shopping analytics system designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior and identify meaningful customer groups.

The system uses **Machine Learning, Data Analytics, and Customer Segmentation** techniques to help businesses understand their customers, discover shopping patterns, and make data-driven marketing decisions.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 👥 Customer Segmentation
- 📊 Shopping & Customer Behavior Analysis
- 🤖 K-Means Clustering
- 📈 Interactive Data Visualization
- 💰 Spending Pattern Analysis
- 🛍️ Purchase Behavior Analysis
- 🔍 Customer Group Identification
- 📋 Customer Analytics Dashboard
- 📌 Data-Driven Business Insights
- 📁 CSV Dataset Upload & Analysis

---

## 🧠 Machine Learning

CartIQ uses the **K-Means Clustering Algorithm** to divide customers into different groups based on their purchasing characteristics.

Customer attributes such as:

- Annual Income
- Spending Score
- Purchase Frequency
- Total Spending
- Age
- Other relevant customer features

can be used for segmentation.

### Example Customer Segments

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| 💎 High-Value Customers | High spending and high purchasing activity |
| 🛍️ Regular Customers | Consistent purchasing behavior |
| 🌱 Potential Customers | Customers with growth potential |
| ⚠️ Low-Engagement Customers | Low spending or low purchase activity |
