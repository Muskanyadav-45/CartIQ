      About CartIQ

      CartIQ groups customers by how they shop. It uses K-Means clustering on attributes such as annual income and spending score, then turns the groups into a dashboard of segments, spending trends, age mix and top products.

      Upload a CSV with at least two numeric columns. Columns named like Age, Category, Month (or Date), Purchase Amount and Rating fill the other charts automatically.
